Qalqalah Kubra Audio Segments for Model Training
==================================================

Total segments: 90
Source: Extracted from last words of ayahs ending with Qaf (ق)
Reciters: 15 different reciters
Surahs: Al-Falaq (113:1-2) and Al-Inshiqaq (84:16-19)

File naming convention:
reciter_sXXXaYYY_qalqalah.wav
Where XXX = surah number, YYY = ayah number

These segments contain the Qalqalah Kubra sound (aq)
from the last word of each ayah, which ends with Qaf.
